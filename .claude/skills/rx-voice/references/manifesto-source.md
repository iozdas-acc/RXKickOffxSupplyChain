## Header

**Rx Manifesto**

## Intro — Rx Principles, Defining Excellence in the AI Age

We realise how difficult the AI revolution is going to be for our customers; we resolve to meet that challenge head on. We will help our customers understand what must change to truly embrace it. We prove this by taking the hardest problems and wielding the new capabilities to drive that revolution. We speak frankly about security, reliability and the hype to keep our focus on the client outcome and delivering transformations that aren't FOMO-driven use cases.

**We don't wait for permission. We don't optimise the status quo. We don't ship theatre.**

We pioneer AI transformation with conviction, velocity, and integrity. We own the outcome. We demand the step change. We compound the win.

---

## Pillar 1 — RX MENTALITY

### First Principles
*We challenge consensus — breakthrough insights are beyond the "best practices."*

- Reject the comfort of "others think" and "this has not been proven"
- Champion independent, unconventional viewpoints that break the norm

**Ask:** Are we solving the actual problem or the comfortable one?

### Proud of the "Bubble"
*We inhabit the Rx bubble with intention — and use that vantage to see what others miss.*

- Recognise the existence of this bubble and apply different perspectives
- Take a balanced view on AI advancement and its associated risks

**Ask:** When change is the constant and pace is the norm, are we still relevant?

### Embedded Ownership
*We sit with the problem and own the result.*

- Work as small, cross-functional squads embedded where the pain is
- No hand-offs: the same team discovers, builds, deploys, and iterates adoption

**Ask:** Who really owns this outcome — and is it us?

---

## Pillar 2 — RX BEHAVIOUR

### Mission First, Everything Else Flexible
*We optimise for outcomes, not outputs.*

- Start from the client's hardest problem and define the mission outcome
- Scope, process, and deliverables are negotiable; the mission is not

**Ask:** Does this choice move us closer to the mission, fast?

### Pioneer Through Ambiguity
*We create the path; we don't wait for someone else to clear it.*

- Move first when the path is unclear — momentum over certainty
- Treat "unproven" as an invitation, not a warning — we exist for the unsolved

**Ask:** If not us, then who? If not now, then when?

### Radical Candour, Collective Intelligence
*We speak plainly because social interaction is the true engine of intelligence.*

- Surface the uncomfortable truths early — polite silence blocks step changes
- Treat conflict as a resource, not a risk — structured disagreement wins

**Ask:** Are we saying what needs to be said, or what's comfortable to hear?

---

## Pillar 3 — RX OBJECTIVES

### Provoke the 10x
*We exist to make inaction untenable.*

- Always bring a bold, evidence-backed point of view
- If it only moves the needle 10%, rethink it

**Ask:** Is this a 10x step change or just an incremental win?

### Build to Learn, Ship to Prove
*We create by shipping small, AI-first prototypes into the world.*

- Go to shipping small, AI-first prototypes into real workflows
- Use data and user behaviour to decide what to keep, kill, or scale

**Ask:** What's the smallest thing we can ship in weeks, not months?

### Codify the Win
*Every mission should raise the floor for the next.*

- Turn solutions into reusable agents, assets, and playbooks by default
- Share openly so the business levels up together, fast

**Ask:** What are we leaving behind that Rx can reuse tomorrow?

## Voice signature (the non-negotiable parts)

1. **We-statements only.** First-person plural. Never "the team" or "you should."
2. **Triple rhythms everywhere.** *Don't wait. Don't optimise. Don't ship theatre.* / *Own. Demand. Compound.*
3. **Italic one-liner below every title.** States the principle as a fact.
4. **Verb-led bullets.** Reject · Champion · Recognise · Take · Work · Start · Move · Treat · Surface · Go · Use · Turn · Share.
5. **"Ask:" always closes.** One probe question, directed at the reader.
