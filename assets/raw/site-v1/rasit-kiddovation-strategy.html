Making It Real

The UK Pilot — Starting Now

A real programme. Real schools. Real outcomes. Designed to be fundable, scalable, and memorable from day one.

Activate

Innovation Labs in UK Schools

Hands-on science and innovation labs delivered in UK schools. Students tackle real challenges — guided by Kidovation facilitators and connected to RASIT’s network of scientists and industry mentors. Creativity, problem-solving, and technology at the core.

Grow

Mentorship & Confidence

Students are matched with mentors from RASIT’s global scientist and expert network. Social dimensions built in: anti-bullying awareness, inclusion, and confidence-building. Every young person leaves with a stronger voice and a clearer sense of possibility.

Elevate

Showcase & Storytelling

A flagship showcase event. Student-led ideas, prototypes, and presentations — made visible at a real leadership audience. Co-branded content captured and shared through RASIT’s Beyond 2030 platform and Accenture Song’s amplification channels.

Connect

From UK Classroom to Global Stage

Selected students earn pathways to RASIT’s international platforms: UN forums, science policy events, and opportunities to be featured in Beyond 2030 magazine — interviewed alongside world leaders and scientists. Scholarships for exceptional participants.
