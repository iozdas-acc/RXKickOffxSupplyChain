# Kidovation — Brief

## Purpose
The dedicated page for the kids hackathon programme. Convert interested parents, teachers, and schools into participants or partners. Show what actually happens at a Kidovation event.

## Audience
- **Primary:** Parents wanting to enrol their child
- **Secondary:** Teachers and schools wanting to run a Kidovation event
- **Tertiary:** Corporate partners wanting to host

## Success criteria
1. Parent/teacher understands what Kidovation is in under 10 seconds
2. They see real evidence it works (photos, numbers, testimonials)
3. Clear path to sign up or get in touch

## Sections / content blocks
1. **Hero** — What is Kidovation? Headline + subhead. Real event photo as background or alongside. Kidovation alien logo prominent.
2. **What kids actually do** — Age range, activities (hackathon format, Lego, alien tech, AI challenges, design thinking). Hands-on, not classroom.
3. **Programs** — Innovation Labs, "Kidovation Week Challenge", Entrepreneurship Bootcamps, STEM Workshops, School Partnerships
4. **A glimpse into our events** — Real event photography gallery (23 photos across 2018, 2019, 2025)
5. **Impact numbers** — 10 years, 12 markets, 3,111+ kids, 115 classrooms
6. **Ready-to-use materials** — Step-by-step illustrated guidance, age-appropriate content, minimal prep time (for teachers)

## Copy tone
Playful and encouraging but backed by real results. Speaks to both the excited kid AND the cautious parent/teacher in the same breath.

## Hard constraints
- Alien character / logo must be prominent — this is Kidovation's identity
- 3D alien hero (D-DS-002) most relevant to this page
- Real event photos only — assets/raw/event-photos/
- Do NOT make it look like a generic kids activity flyer
