# Home — Brief

## Purpose
Introduce Innovation X as the umbrella ecosystem housing Kidovation and Future Labs. Orient the visitor instantly and route them to the right sub-brand. First impression — must feel bold, energetic, and real.

## Audience
Mixed — parents, teachers, corporate partners, potential volunteers, curious kids. All arrive knowing the name but little else.

## Success criteria
1. Visitor understands Innovation X = umbrella → Kidovation (kids) + Future Labs (teens/young people)
2. Visitor clicks through to the sub-brand relevant to them
3. Visitor feels this is credible, energetic, and not like every other kids education site

## Sections / content blocks
1. **Hero** — Headline: "Inspiring the next generation." Subheadline introducing Innovation X as ecosystem. Two CTAs: Explore Kidovation / Discover Future Labs. 3D alien character confirmed (D-DS-002 accepted) — R3F, green alien primary, fallback to 2D if WebGL unavailable.
2. **What is Innovation X?** — Short explanation: parent ecosystem, focus on innovation/education/creativity/future-building. Visual diagram: Innovation X → Kidovation → Future Labs.
3. **Our Ecosystem** — Side-by-side: Kidovation (kids, playful, hackathon) vs Future Labs (teens, practical, advanced). Each with mission + CTA.
4. **Mission & Vision** — Full vision statement. Real photography from events.
5. **A glimpse into our events** — Real event photos grid (assets/raw/event-photos/).

## Copy tone
Bold and energetic. Not corporate. Not childish. Backs every claim with real evidence. Short sentences.

## Hard constraints
- Real photos only — use assets/raw/event-photos/
- 3D alien confirmed (D-DS-002 accepted) — hero uses R3F alien, must have 2D fallback
- Must show Innovation X as umbrella without burying Kidovation (the known brand)
- Something to show Matt P — immediate priority
