# Future Labs — Brief

## Purpose
The dedicated page for the teen / young adult programme. More advanced, more professional, science/tech vibe. R&D, tech incubation, experimental learning. Bridges Kidovation graduates into the next stage.

## Audience
- Teenagers (16+) interested in advanced innovation and tech
- Schools and colleges looking for a step up from basic STEM
- Corporate sponsors interested in the talent pipeline angle

## Success criteria
1. Visitor immediately understands this is different from Kidovation — more advanced, for older cohort
2. Feels credible enough for teens who'd roll their eyes at "kids stuff"
3. Clear CTA to explore or get involved

## Sections / content blocks
1. **Hero** — What is Future Labs? Rocket-flask logo prominent. Neon/sci-fi energy from brand doc.
2. **Mission & Vision** — Future Labs mission (practical, hands-on, AI + human-centred design + creative problem-solving for future of work)
3. **What participants do** — Advanced workshops, R&D challenges, tech incubation, experimental learning
4. **Page** — Additional content TBD pending more input from client

## Copy tone
More professional than Kidovation. Still energetic. Speaks to a teenager who wants to be taken seriously — not talked down to, not over-hyped.

## Hard constraints
- Visually distinct from Kidovation — Future Labs = cooler, more advanced, less cartoon
- Rocket-flask logo is the visual anchor
- Colour is less vibrant than Kidovation per brand doc (P-COLOR-002)
- Content for this page is less defined — flag as needing more client input
