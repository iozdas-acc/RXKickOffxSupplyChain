# The Procurement Transformation — Brief

## Purpose

Tell the story of the 12-week procurement engagement: what was done, how AI was embedded, and what it produced. This is the evidence page — it transforms the proof numbers from Chapter 1 into a concrete, followable story.

## Audience

Same stakeholders as Chapter 1, now engaged. They want the specifics: what did the project look like, how was AI actually used (not just claimed), and what did the outputs look like compared to the traditional approach?

## Success criteria

1. Visitor understands the structure and scope of the 12-week procurement project.
2. Visitor sees how AI tools (Horizons Thinking, Sarah Website, Pain Point Navigator) were embedded — not bolted on, but woven into the delivery.
3. Visitor leaves with a clear picture of the output quality uplift vs. traditional approach.

## Sections / content blocks

1. **Chapter indicator** — "02 / 05"
2. **Section headline** — frames the project: "The 12-Week Procurement Transformation"
3. **Project overview** — brief scope statement: enterprise procurement, traditional structure, RX-led, AI-embedded throughout.
4. **AI tools embedded** — the three tools surfaced as distinct moments: Horizons Thinking / Sarah Website / Pain Point Navigator. One line on what each did — not a product feature list, a delivery proof point.
5. **Outcome** — higher-quality outputs than the traditional approach. [Specific evidence TBD from Raj/Ben]. Framing: "same scope, better result."
6. **Transition to tension** — the setup for what comes next: "The outcomes were strong. The model had a problem." → advance to Chapter 3.

## Copy tone

Factual and specific. This page earns its claims with detail. No vague consulting language ("we partnered to deliver transformative outcomes"). Name the tools, name the approach, name the result.

## Hard constraints

- Do not overstate AI's role — it was embedded within a traditional structure, not a fully autonomous delivery
- The three AI tools (Horizons Thinking, Sarah Website, Pain Point Navigator) must each appear — they are the proof of RX's differentiation
- Output quality claim must be supported with evidence from Raj/Ben before copy is finalised
