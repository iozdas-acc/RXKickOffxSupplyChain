# Work With Us — Brief

## Purpose
Convert interested schools, NGOs, corporates, and volunteers into active partners. The action page — everything leads here.

## Audience
- Schools wanting to run Kidovation in their classrooms
- Corporate partners wanting to host or sponsor events
- NGOs wanting to bring Kidovation to underserved communities
- Parents and individuals wanting to volunteer or get involved

## Success criteria
1. Each audience type immediately sees a path made for them
2. Getting in touch feels easy and low-friction
3. The value proposition for each partner type is crystal clear

## Sections / content blocks
1. **If you are a school** — Ready-to-use materials, minimal prep, step-by-step guidance, ran events in classrooms. CTA: Partner with us.
2. **If you are a partner / corporate** — Host an event, sponsor, bring Kidovation to your clients and employees. Accenture Song credibility. CTA: Let's talk.
3. **If you are a parent** — How to get your child involved. Upcoming events. CTA: Find an event.
4. **Get in touch** — Simple contact form or email. Low friction.

## Copy tone
Direct and action-oriented. Each section speaks only to that audience — no generic "contact us" padding.

## Hard constraints
- Must have clearly separated audience paths — schools / partners / parents
- CTA on every section — no dead ends
- Keep it simple — this is a conversion page, not a brochure
