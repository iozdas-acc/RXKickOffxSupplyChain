# About Us — Brief

## Purpose
Tell the story of Innovation X / Kidovation — why it exists, how it grew, who built it. Human, generational, credible. This page turns curiosity into trust.

## Audience
Partners, sponsors, schools, NGOs evaluating whether to work with us. Also talent considering joining.

## Success criteria
1. Visitor understands the 10-year journey and feels the weight of it
2. Visitor connects with the team — sees real people, not a corporate programme
3. Visitor understands the generational structure and feels the continuity

## Sections / content blocks
1. **Our Story / Philosophy / Values** — Innovation X's journey from 2015. Why it exists. Learning philosophy. Values in practice. Timeline (2015 → 2025, from brand deck).
2. **Leadership / Founders** — Generational team structure:
   - Gen 1 (Founder): Fiona Russell — headshot + 1–2 sentence bio + area of expertise + personal motivation
   - Gen 2 (Leads): Anastasia Palchikova, Christine Yu, Hannah Stephens — same format
   - Gen 3 (Current): Izzie Ozdas, Jack Riley, Azeez Adebayo — same format
   - NOT a flat grid — show the generational hierarchy visually (P-SPACE-002)
3. **Brand is evolving / growing** — How the brand has developed across generations

## Copy tone
Warm and human. Personal motivations for each team member — this humanises the brand. Not a corporate "meet the team" page.

## Hard constraints
- Real headshots only — assets/raw/team/ (7 photos)
- Generational structure must be visually distinct — not a flat 7-person grid
- Bio format: 1–2 sentence summary + area of expertise + personal motivation/belief
