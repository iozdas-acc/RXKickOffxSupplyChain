## What it is

Innovation X is the umbrella ecosystem. One website, three brands:
- **Kidovation** — hands-on hackathon for kids (primary known brand, alien mascot)
- **Future Labs** — advanced programme for teens 16+ (rocket-flask, sci-fi vibe)
- **Innovation X** — parent brand, orbiting X logo

Founded 2015 by Accenture Song. 10 years, 12 markets, 3,111+ kids, 115 classrooms.

**Vision:** Empower young people to grow into confident, creative thinkers who dare to imagine and innovate for a better world.
