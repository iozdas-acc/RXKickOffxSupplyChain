## Target audience

Multiple audiences — the site must serve all of them:
- **Kids (primary participants):** young people attending hackathons and events — energetic, curious, creative
- **Parents & teachers:** decision-makers who enrol kids — want credibility, safety, outcomes
- **Corporate partners & NGOs:** Accenture clients, schools, hospitals, NGOs who host or fund Kidovation
- **Potential team members / volunteers:** next generation of Kidovation leads

Context: Accenture Song initiative (UK). Events run in schools, children's hospitals, corporate offices, remote areas.

---

## Use cases

1. Understand what Kidovation is and sign up / get involved
2. See the impact — real stories, real numbers, real kids
3. Find out how to partner or bring Kidovation to their organisation
4. Meet the team and understand the generational structure

---

## Brand personality & tone

**Bold, playful, and real — not corporate, not fluffy.**
- Energy of a hackathon room full of excited kids — but credible enough for Accenture
- Warm and human, not polished and sterile
- Confident about the mission — 10 years of impact backs it up
- Direct. Not watered-down CSR language.

Visual direction clues from assets:
- Strong use of blue/purple and bright yellow (seen in deck and event screen)
- Bold typography with clear hierarchy
- Real photography only — kids in action, not posed stock
- The "yellow sticky note" treatment in the deck suggests intentional informality alongside the structure

**NOT:** AI-generated icons, pastel gradients, glassmorphism, rounded card grids, fluffy illustration

---

## What makes this unforgettable?

10 years of real kids, real hackathons, real impact — shown through real photography and a generational team that has grown up with the programme.

---

## Hard constraints

- Must not look AI-generated — real photos, intentional design choices only
- No stock photography — real event photos and real headshots only
- Accenture Song origin must be acknowledged but Kidovation is its own brand
- FY26 goal: rebuild foundations and strengthen brand — this site IS that rebuild
- Self-sustainable target: FY27 — brand needs to stand on its own
